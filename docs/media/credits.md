# Launch media and provenance

- Motion design and renderer: original composition authored for Phone MCP, MIT licensed with the repository.
- Format: 1920×1080, 30 fps, 32 seconds, H.264 video with AAC audio. The final measured metadata is recorded separately after rendering.
- Music: **Phone MCP — Command in Motion**, generated in the maintainer's existing [Google Flow Music](https://www.flowmusic.app/) account on 21 October 2026. [Generation page](https://www.flowmusic.app/song/c24bb286-e69f-432a-a15d-fff3d38fdb3e). The generated source is approximately 66 seconds; the film uses the first 32 seconds with loudness processing and an ending fade.
- Music prompt: instrumental electronic launch soundtrack, 140 BPM direction, punchy breakbeats, tight sub bass, arpeggiated synth hook, tactile glitch percussion, immediate groove and stop-start edits. The requested tempo is artistic direction, not an independently measured tempo claim.
- Android session/files images: actual 0.8.2 app captures in an isolated emulator. The settings page deliberately blocks screenshots with Android `FLAG_SECURE` and is not reproduced.
- Calculator image: actual Samsung S24 Ultra capture through Phone MCP in Codex, after verifying `137 × 24 = 3 288` on its separate display. No private conversations or notifications were used.
- Flow diagrams, example notifications, transfer progress and AI-client graphics are illustrative motion design. They are not unedited screen recordings of Chat, Work, Codex, a notification send or an APK installation.
- Fonts: locally installed Arial/Consolas (or DejaVu fallbacks). Font files are not redistributed.
