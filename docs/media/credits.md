# Launch media and provenance

- Motion design and renderer: original Remotion/React composition authored for Phone MCP, MIT licensed with the repository. It replaces the initial Python-rendered film. [Editable project and render command](remotion/README.md).
- Format: 1920×1080, 30 fps, 22 seconds, H.264 video with AAC audio. Measured metadata and the full decode result are in `verification.json`.
- Workflow and motion references: [Liamrjohnston/remotion-motion-graphics-skill](https://github.com/Liamrjohnston/remotion-motion-graphics-skill), principally its continuous pipeline camera and authentic working-surface mechanisms. The four skills were installed, and their authentic-asset/reference preflight and independent critic gates were executed. The skill's MIT notice is included in `remotion/licenses/`.
- Independent visual review: all three sections passed at an average of 8.5/10 after corrections. Review used the actual MP4s, close transition frames and authentic assets; it did not include real-time perceptual playback or an audio listening assessment.
- Music: **Phone MCP — Command in Motion**, generated in the maintainer's existing [Google Flow Music](https://www.flowmusic.app/) account on 21 October 2026. [Generation page](https://www.flowmusic.app/song/c24bb286-e69f-432a-a15d-fff3d38fdb3e). The generated source is approximately 66 seconds; the film uses the first 22 seconds with loudness processing and a 1.2-second ending fade.
- Music prompt: instrumental electronic launch soundtrack, 140 BPM direction, punchy breakbeats, tight sub bass, arpeggiated synth hook, tactile glitch percussion, immediate groove and stop-start edits. The requested tempo is artistic direction, not an independently measured tempo claim.
- Android session/files images: actual 0.8.2 app captures in an isolated emulator. The settings page deliberately blocks screenshots with Android `FLAG_SECURE` and is not reproduced.
- Calculator image: actual Samsung S24 Ultra capture through Phone MCP in Codex, after verifying `137 × 24 = 3 288` on its separate display. No private conversations or notifications were used.
- The separated display planes, tool routes, notification symbol and AI-client branches are illustrative motion design. They are not unedited screen recordings of Chat, Work, Codex, a notification send or an APK installation. Session and Files show their actual stopped/paused state; no successful transfer is fabricated.
- Fonts: Inter Tight and IBM Plex Mono, supplied by pinned Fontsource packages under SIL Open Font Licenses. License texts are included; source font binaries are installed from npm rather than vendored.
